One movement,
four readings.

A single limb moves in the middle. Around it, four notation systems — Labanotation, Benesh, Eshkol‑Wachman and Motif — write down the same gesture their own way. One playhead drives all five. Each window isolates one axis where the four disagree in kind; in every window exactly one reading is inert — it cannot see the difference, and says so.

What you are looking at

The four systems are not translations of one another; they carry different things well. This page holds the movement fixed and lets each system spell it, so the differences are differences in kind, not in accuracy. The single-limb figure uses the same abstraction as the Studio and Parameters pages — every segment is a direction on a sphere.
