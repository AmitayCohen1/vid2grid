What you are looking at

The four systems are not translations of one another; they carry different things well. This page holds the movement fixed and lets each system spell it, so the differences are differences in kind, not in accuracy. The single-limb figure uses the same abstraction as the Studio and Parameters pages — every segment is a direction on a sphere.
